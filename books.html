📚 Books

⚖️ Editorial & Publishing Principles

🔎 Accuracy

Factual material should be carefully reviewed and supported by appropriate sources.

🧭 Context

Complex subjects should be presented with enough context to support meaningful understanding.

❤️ Dignity

People should never be reduced to sensational subjects for publication.

🔐 Privacy

Sensitive personal information should be handled carefully and responsibly.

🛡️ Safety

Publishing should not create unnecessary risks for authors, participants or communities.

©️ Attribution

Authors, photographers, researchers and other contributors should receive appropriate recognition.

🤖 Publishing & Technology

Digital tools are changing how books are written, produced, translated, distributed and accessed.

Human Creativity Remains Central

Technology may assist with editing, accessibility, translation, research organization and production. Human authorship, editorial judgment and responsibility remain central to the publishing process.

🧠 AI & Books

Emerging AI systems may become useful tools for authors and publishers, while also creating important questions about authorship, originality and responsible use.

✍️ Human Authorship

Human creative direction and authorship should remain clearly recognized.

🔎 Transparency

Significant AI assistance should be communicated responsibly where appropriate.

©️ Creative Rights

Publishing should respect applicable rights, attribution and intellectual property.

⚖️ Responsible Use

Technology should support creativity and access without replacing human responsibility.
