🧭 Audio Production Process

A responsible audio production combines creative direction with careful recording, editing and review.

1️⃣ Concept

Define the subject, purpose, audience and creative direction of the production.

2️⃣ Preparation

Prepare scripts, questions, recording environments, participants and technical requirements.

3️⃣ Recording

Capture voices, music, interviews, performances and environmental sound.

4️⃣ Editing

Organize and edit recorded material while preserving its intended meaning.

5️⃣ Mixing

Balance voices, music, effects and other elements for a clear listening experience.

6️⃣ Review

Check accuracy, context, privacy, rights, safety and final audio quality before publication.

❤️ Human Voices

A recorded voice carries personal experience, emotion and identity. Recording another person's voice therefore carries responsibility.

People Before Audio

Participants should be treated with dignity and respect. Sensitive conversations should be handled carefully, with attention to privacy, safety, consent and context.
