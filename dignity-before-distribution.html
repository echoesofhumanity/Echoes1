❤️ Dignity Before Distribution

Human dignity must come before visibility, publicity, engagement and distribution.

Echoes of Humanity believes that humanitarian communication must never turn a person's suffering, vulnerability or personal story into a commodity.

🌍 The Principle

Distribution can bring a story to millions of people. But reach is never more important than the person whose story is being communicated.

Before a photograph is published, a video is distributed, an interview is broadcast or a story is shared, the human consequences of that communication must be considered.

❤️

People Before Distribution

The purpose of communication is not to expose people. It is to help people be understood without sacrificing their dignity, privacy, safety or humanity.

🧭 Why Dignity Comes First

❤️ Human Beings Are Not Content

People experiencing hardship should never be reduced to images, statistics, headlines or engagement opportunities.

🔐 Privacy Matters

Personal information, identities and sensitive circumstances should be protected whenever possible.

🛡️ Safety Matters

Publication should not create unnecessary danger for individuals, families or communities.

🧭 Context Matters

A powerful image or short statement can become misleading when separated from its circumstances.

📷 Before Publishing an Image

Images can communicate powerful emotions. That power requires responsibility.

1️⃣ Ask Who Is Shown

Consider the identity, circumstances and vulnerability of the people represented.

2️⃣ Ask What Is Revealed

Consider whether the image exposes private, sensitive or potentially harmful information.

3️⃣ Ask What It Communicates

Consider whether the image provides meaningful understanding or simply creates an emotional reaction.

4️⃣ Ask Whether It Is Necessary

A powerful image is not automatically a necessary image.

🎥 Before Distributing Video

Video can reveal voices, faces, locations, relationships and circumstances that may remain hidden in other formats.

🎙️ Interviews

Participants should understand how their interviews may be used and distributed.

📍 Locations

Location information should not unnecessarily expose vulnerable people or communities.

✂️ Editing

Editing should not deliberately distort a person's meaning or create a misleading impression.

🌐 Distribution

The wider the distribution, the greater the potential consequences for the people represented.

🎙️ Before Sharing a Personal Story

Personal stories belong first to the people who lived them. Communication should respect that ownership of experience.

🤝 Consent

Appropriate consent should be considered before publishing identifiable personal stories.

🧠 Understanding

People should understand the context in which their story will be communicated.

❤️ Respect

Storytelling should preserve the humanity of the person rather than focus only on suffering.

🛡️ Protection

Vulnerable individuals may require additional protection from identification or exposure.

🚫 What We Should Avoid

🚫 Sensationalism

Avoid exaggeration, shocking presentation and unnecessary emotional manipulation.

🚫 Exploitation

Never use another person's vulnerability simply to increase attention or engagement.

🚫 Dehumanization

People should never be represented only through poverty, suffering, trauma or helplessness.

🚫 Misleading Context

Do not remove essential context in ways that change the meaning of a person's experience.

🚫 Unnecessary Exposure

Do not reveal identities, locations or personal details when doing so creates avoidable risk.

🚫 Engagement Before Humanity

Views, shares, likes and reach should never become more important than the person represented.

🌐 Distribution Responsibility

Distribution is not a neutral technical action. Once information enters a public network, it may be copied, translated, edited, archived and redistributed indefinitely.

📱 Social Media

Short-form distribution should not remove essential context or encourage harmful exposure.

📺 Television

Broadcast material should respect participants and provide appropriate context.

🌐 Digital Platforms

Online distribution can expand reach rapidly and therefore requires careful consideration.

📚 Archives

Archiving can preserve material for years, making responsible original publication especially important.

🧑‍💼 Editorial Responsibility

Editors, journalists, filmmakers, photographers, writers, producers and creators all share responsibility for how human stories are presented.

🔎 Verify

Check important factual claims and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

🧭 Provide Context

Give audiences enough information to understand the wider circumstances.

❤️ Protect Dignity

Consider the person before considering the impact of the content.

🛡️ Assess Risk

Consider foreseeable consequences before publication and distribution.

🤖 Technology & AI

Digital tools and AI systems can assist with translation, editing, organization and distribution, but technological capability does not remove human responsibility.

🧠 Human Oversight

Human judgment should remain essential for ethical, editorial and creative decisions.

🔎 Verification

AI-assisted material should be reviewed before publication when accuracy or human representation is important.

🔐 Sensitive Information

Personal and sensitive material requires particular care when processed through digital systems.

❤️ Human Meaning

Technology should serve human communication rather than replace responsibility toward people.

🧭 The Dignity Test

Before distributing a story, image, recording or video, ask a simple question:

❤️

If this were my story, would I want it communicated this way?

If the answer is uncertain, pause. Reconsider the framing, context, consent, privacy and potential impact before distribution.

🌍 A Shared Standard

Dignity Before Distribution is not limited to one medium. It applies to photography, video, audio, journalism, documentaries, publications, social media and every other form of human communication.

📷 Photography

People before the image.

🎥 Video

People before the footage.

🎧 Audio

People before the recording.

📰 Journalism

People before the headline.

📱 Social Media

People before engagement.

📚 Publications

People before circulation.
