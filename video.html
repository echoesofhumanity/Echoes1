Responsible video production combines storytelling, research, technical preparation and human judgment.

📝

Story Development

Stories are developed with attention to context, accuracy, purpose and the people represented.

Production

Technology exists to support humanitarian storytelling, not to replace human judgment, responsibility or ethics.

Every video must respect human dignity, privacy, safety, consent, context and the people represented in the story.
