📷 Photo Archive

The visual archive of Echoes of Humanity.

The Photo Archive preserves photographs that document people, communities, places, events, humanitarian action, cultural activity and creative work across the Echoes of Humanity network.

🌍 About the Photo Archive

Photography can preserve moments, communicate experiences and provide visual context for stories that might otherwise remain unseen.

The Echoes of Humanity Photo Archive is designed to preserve visual material while keeping dignity, privacy, consent, accuracy and context at the centre of photographic work.

🔎 Archive Organization

A growing visual archive requires clear organization so that photographs can be discovered without losing their original context.

📅 Date

Photographs may be organized according to the date on which they were created or documented.

📍 Location

Geographic information may help connect photographs to their wider context.

🏷️ Subject

Subjects and themes can help users discover relevant visual stories.

📷 Photographer

Appropriate creator attribution should be preserved wherever available.

🔐 Privacy & Consent

Photographs involving identifiable people require particular care. The archive should consider consent, privacy, safety, context and the potential consequences of publication.

🔐 Privacy

Personal information should not be exposed unnecessarily through photographic material.

🤝 Consent

Appropriate consent and permissions should be considered when photographs are created or published.

🛡️ Safety

Publication should not create avoidable risks for vulnerable people or communities.

❤️ Dignity

Images should not exploit suffering or reduce people to sensational visual material.

©️ Photography Rights

Photographs are creative works and should be treated with appropriate respect for intellectual property and creator rights.

📷 Creator Credit

Photographers should receive appropriate attribution when their work is used.

©️ Copyright

Photographs should not be reproduced or redistributed without appropriate authorization.

📝 Editorial Use

Approved images may be made available for specific editorial or public-interest purposes.

🌐 Responsible Sharing

Sharing should preserve attribution, context and the intended meaning of the original work.
