🤝 Solidarity

🌱 Hope & Resilience

🧭 Responsible Photography

❤️ Dignity

People should never be photographed or presented merely as objects of suffering or spectacle.

🔐 Privacy

Sensitive personal information and circumstances should be handled with care.

🛡️ Safety

Photography should not create unnecessary risks for individuals or communities.

🧭 Context

Images should be presented with enough context to avoid misleading audiences.

🔎 Authenticity

Documentary images should not be manipulated in ways that fundamentally misrepresent reality.

©️ Attribution

Photographers and contributors should receive appropriate recognition for their work.

🗣️ Local Voices

🌐 Cultural Exchange

💻 Photography in the Digital Age

Digital technology has transformed how photographs are created, edited, distributed and preserved.

Technology Should Serve the Story

Digital tools can improve production and accessibility, but technology should not replace authenticity, context, creative judgment or human responsibility.

🤖 AI & Visual Creation

Emerging AI systems are creating new possibilities for visual experimentation while also raising important questions about authorship, authenticity and representation.

🧑‍🎨 Human Direction

Human creative direction and judgment should remain central to artistic work.

🔎 Transparency

Significant synthetic or AI-assisted visual content should be communicated responsibly where appropriate.

©️ Creative Rights

Digital creation should respect applicable rights, authorship and attribution.

⚖️ Responsible Use

New technology should expand creativity without unnecessarily harming artists or communities.
