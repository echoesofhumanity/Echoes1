❤️ Humanitarian Writing

Humanitarian articles should communicate difficult realities without reducing people to statistics or sensational stories.

People Before Headlines

Every humanitarian story should consider the dignity, privacy, safety and context of the people represented. The goal is understanding rather than exploitation.

⚖️ Editorial Principles

Echoes of Humanity articles are built around responsible communication and public-interest values.

🔎 Verification

Important factual claims should be checked against reliable sources and available evidence.

🧭 Context

Articles should provide enough background to help readers understand the circumstances surrounding an event or issue.

❤️ Human Dignity

People should never be treated merely as subjects of attention or content.

🔐 Privacy

Sensitive personal information should be handled carefully and responsibly.

🛡️ Safety

Publication should not create unnecessary risks for vulnerable people, sources or communities.

⚖️ Fairness

Different relevant perspectives should be represented honestly and responsibly.

🔬 Research & Background

Strong articles often begin with careful research. Background information, primary material and reliable sources can help create more accurate public communication.

📚 Source Research

Relevant documents, records and reliable sources should be examined whenever appropriate.

📊 Evidence

Evidence should be distinguished from assumptions, interpretation and unverified claims.

🧠 Analysis

Complex issues should be examined carefully rather than reduced to simplistic conclusions.

🔗 Source Transparency

Where appropriate, readers should be able to understand the basis for important claims.

🧭 Responsible Article Process

1️⃣ Research

Identify the subject, relevant questions, available evidence and reliable sources.

2️⃣ Understand

Examine the wider social, historical and human context surrounding the subject.

3️⃣ Write

Present information clearly while distinguishing facts, analysis and opinion.

4️⃣ Review

Check accuracy, context, privacy, safety and potential harm before publication.

5️⃣ Publish

Publish responsibly and make corrections when meaningful errors are identified.

6️⃣ Learn

Reader feedback, new evidence and developments can contribute to future updates and understanding.
