Pinterest Content Operations

Pinterest will serve as a visual discovery and knowledge-sharing channel within the Echoes of Humanity communication network.

📥

Content Intake

Visual stories, educational resources, campaign materials, photographs, graphics and creative ideas enter the system.

🔎

Review & Verification

Visual material is reviewed for accuracy, context, dignity, rights and responsible humanitarian communication.

🧭

Classification

Visual resources can be organized by topic, campaign, project, educational purpose, audience and region.

📌

Visual Collections

Related humanitarian resources can be organized into meaningful visual collections and boards.

🎓

Educational Resources

Infographics, learning materials, guides and visual explanations can support humanitarian education.

📣

Campaign Boards

Humanitarian campaigns can be presented through coordinated visual collections and awareness materials.

🎨

Creative Arts

Photography, illustration, design and creative work can communicate humanitarian values through visual storytelling.

🌍

Global Stories

Visual stories from different cultures, communities and regions can be connected through shared humanitarian themes.

🤖

AI Assistance

Future AI systems may assist with classification, translation, descriptions, organization and content preparation.

🧠

Human Review

AI assistance does not replace human responsibility. Sensitive visual material remains subject to human review.

📝

Platform Preparation

Visual content can be adapted for Pinterest formats, descriptions, collections and audience needs.

Publishing Queue
