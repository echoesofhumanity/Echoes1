✍️ Writers

Writers, journalists, poets and storytellers giving language to human experiences, ideas and memories.

Echoes of Humanity Writers creates a space where words can inform, preserve memory, encourage understanding and connect people across cultures.

📖 The Power of Words

Writing can preserve a moment, explain a complex subject, document an experience or simply help another person understand a life different from their own.

❤️ Writing & Humanitarian Stories

Writing can help audiences understand humanitarian realities without reducing people to statistics or headlines.

People Before the Story

When writing about real people, the story must remain connected to the dignity, safety, privacy and humanity of those whose experiences are being represented.

📰 Responsible Journalism

🔎 Verification

Factual claims should be checked against reliable sources and available evidence.

🧭 Context

Important stories should contain enough context to reduce misunderstanding.

🔐 Privacy

Sensitive personal information should be handled carefully and responsibly.

❤️ Dignity

People should never be reduced to sensational subjects or attention-generating content.

⚖️ Fairness

Different perspectives should be represented honestly and responsibly.

🛡️ Safety

Publishing should not create unnecessary risks for writers, sources or communities.
