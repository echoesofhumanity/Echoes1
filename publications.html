Publications

Knowledge, research and human stories preserved through responsible publishing.

The Echoes of Humanity Publications Network brings together articles, reports, research, magazines, books, essays and other written works connected to humanitarian communication and public knowledge.

Knowledge Has a Memory

Important ideas and human experiences should not disappear when a conversation ends or a news cycle moves on.

Publications create a lasting record that can be read, studied, shared and returned to by future generations. Echoes of Humanity therefore treats publishing as part of its wider humanitarian knowledge infrastructure.

Editorial Process

💡

Idea Development

🔎

Research

📝

Writing & Editing

Material is developed and edited for clarity, accuracy, accessibility and responsible communication.

👤

Human Review

Human editorial review remains essential, especially when material involves sensitive subjects or vulnerable people.

⚖️

Responsible Publishing

Publishing has consequences. Information should be communicated in a way that protects dignity, reduces misinformation and respects the people represented.

❤️

Human Dignity

People should never be reduced to statistics, headlines or sensational narratives.

🔐

Privacy

Personal and sensitive information must be handled with appropriate care and responsibility.

🔎

Accuracy

Published information should be researched, reviewed and presented honestly.

⚖️

Context

Readers deserve enough context to understand complex subjects without misleading simplification.
