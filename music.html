Echoes of Humanity
Music

Music as memory, expression, connection and a universal language of humanity.

The Echoes of Humanity Music Network brings together original compositions, humanitarian sound projects, artists, performances, sound archives and music created to accompany stories, documentaries and human experiences.

Music & Humanity

Music can carry emotions and memories across borders, languages and generations. Within Echoes of Humanity, music is treated as a form of human communication rather than simply entertainment.

Original works may accompany humanitarian stories, educational projects, documentaries, campaigns and moments of remembrance while preserving the dignity of the people and stories represented.
