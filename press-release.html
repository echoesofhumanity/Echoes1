⚖️ Editorial Standard

Official communications should be prepared with clarity, accuracy, context and respect for the people involved.

🔎 Verification

Significant factual information should be checked before publication whenever appropriate.

🧭 Context

Announcements should provide enough background to help audiences understand their significance.

🔐 Privacy

Personal and sensitive information should be handled carefully and responsibly.

❤️ Dignity

People and communities should never be used as sensational material for communication.

📝 Press Release Structure

Press releases may follow a consistent structure to make important information easy to understand and reference.

📅 Date

The publication date of the official announcement.

📍 Location

Where appropriate, the geographic or organizational context of the announcement.

📰 Headline

A clear and informative description of the announcement.

📖 Summary

A concise explanation of the most important information.

📚 Context

Relevant background information supporting understanding.

📨 Media Contact

Appropriate official contact information for journalists and media inquiries.

🧑‍💼 For Journalists & Media

Journalists and media organizations may use official press releases as a source of organizational information while maintaining independent editorial verification.

🔎 Verification Before Publication

Press releases represent official communications from Echoes of Humanity. Journalists remain responsible for independent verification, source assessment and editorial judgment when using information in published reporting.
