❤️ Humanitarian News

Human-centered journalism focused on humanitarian action, dignity, protection and the realities experienced by people and communities around the world.

Stories are approached with accuracy, context, responsibility and respect for the people represented.

Human-Centered Journalism

Humanitarian journalism is not only about reporting events. It is about understanding the people behind those events, the communities affected and the human consequences of decisions, conflicts, disasters and social change.

❤️ Human Dignity

Every story should preserve the dignity, privacy and humanity of the people represented.

🔎 Context

Humanitarian events should be presented with sufficient context to help people understand what happened and why it matters.

🛡️ Protection

Reporting must consider the safety and protection of vulnerable people, children and communities.

🧠 Responsible Reporting

Information should be reviewed carefully before being communicated to the public.

🌍 Humanitarian Stories

Stories from communities, humanitarian workers, volunteers, researchers and people whose experiences deserve to be heard.

📰 Global News

International developments and humanitarian stories affecting people and communities.

🌍 Regional Stories

Local and regional humanitarian stories connected to the wider global picture.

🚨 Breaking Developments

Important humanitarian developments requiring rapid, careful and responsible communication.

🔎 Investigative Journalism

Research-based reporting examining complex humanitarian and social issues.

👥 People Before Headlines

Behind every humanitarian story are real people. Their lives, experiences, losses, hopes and voices should never become secondary to the news itself.

🧒 Children

Children require particular care, protection and ethical consideration in humanitarian reporting.

🏘️ Communities

Communities should be represented with context, accuracy and respect for their identity.

🤝 Humanitarian Workers

The experiences of people working to protect and assist others are an important part of humanitarian journalism.

🗣️ Human Voices

Whenever possible, stories should make space for people to speak about their own experiences.

🧭 Editorial Principles

Humanitarian News follows a human-centered editorial approach built around accuracy, responsibility, transparency and dignity.

✔️ Accuracy

Information should be checked and presented as accurately as possible.

⚖️ Fairness

Reporting should avoid unnecessary bias, sensationalism and dehumanizing language.

🔐 Privacy

Personal information and sensitive circumstances should be handled with care.

🌍 Context

Important stories should be understood within their social, historical and humanitarian context.
