📡 Streaming

A digital environment for live and on-demand Echoes of Humanity programs, conversations, performances and humanitarian events.

Streaming connects audiences with stories and experiences from different places while keeping responsible communication, accessibility and human dignity at the center.

♿ Accessibility

Digital streaming should be designed so that people with different abilities, languages and communication needs can access meaningful content.

📝 Captions

Captions can improve access to conversations, programs and recorded broadcasts.

🎧 Audio Access

Audio formats can provide alternative ways to experience visual or written material.

🌍 Translation

Responsible translation can help stories reach audiences across languages and regions.

📱 Device Access

Streaming environments should work across commonly used digital devices where possible.

🤝 Community Streaming

Streaming can help local voices reach audiences beyond geographical boundaries.

Local Voices, Global Audience

Community events, local discussions, cultural programs and humanitarian activities may eventually be shared through the Echoes streaming network while respecting local context and participant safety.

⚖️ Editorial Responsibility

Streaming is not simply a technical distribution system. Live and recorded content can affect real people and communities.

🔎 Verification

Important factual information should be checked whenever verification is possible and appropriate.

🔐 Privacy

Sensitive personal information should be protected during recording and distribution.

🛡️ Safety

Participants should not be placed at unnecessary risk because of a broadcast.

❤️ Dignity

People should never be reduced to sensational content simply to increase attention.

🧭 Context

Programs should preserve enough context to help audiences understand what they are seeing.

👤 Human Moderation

Sensitive live environments require appropriate human oversight and moderation.

💻 Streaming Technology

📡 Live Delivery

🗂️ Media Archive

🤖 AI Assistance

Future systems may assist with transcription, translation, classification and accessibility.

👤 Human Oversight

Human editorial judgment remains essential for sensitive content and important decisions.
