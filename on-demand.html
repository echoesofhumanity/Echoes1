▶️ On Demand

A future archive for Echoes TV programs, interviews, documentaries and visual storytelling.

Echoes On Demand is designed to give audiences the freedom to discover, watch and revisit meaningful programs whenever they choose.

📺 Watch When You Choose

Important stories should not disappear simply because their original broadcast has ended. On Demand is intended to create a lasting home for Echoes TV productions.

🎬 Programs

Original Echoes TV programs available for audiences to discover and revisit.

🎙️ Interviews

Conversations with people, researchers, practitioners, creators and communities.

🎥 Documentaries

Long-form visual stories exploring people, communities and humanitarian realities.

🗣️ Debates

Recorded discussions examining important questions affecting humanity.

♿ Accessible Media

On Demand should make meaningful content accessible to as many people as possible.

📝 Transcripts

Written transcripts can help audiences access conversations and programs in another format.

💬 Captions

Captions can improve access to visual content for people who are deaf or hard of hearing.

🌐 Languages

Future multilingual support can help stories reach audiences across language boundaries.

🔊 Audio Access

Audio alternatives can provide additional ways to experience visual storytelling.

❤️ Responsible Archive

An archive must protect both the value of the story and the dignity of the people represented in it.

People Before Content

Content may remain available for years, but ethical responsibility does not disappear with time. Privacy, dignity, safety, context and appropriate access remain important throughout the life of an archive.
