🧠 Human Review

Human judgment remains at the centre of responsible communication.

Human Review is the process through which important content, decisions and media materials are examined by people before publication, distribution or wider use.

🌍 Why Human Review Matters

Technology can accelerate research, production, translation, editing and distribution. It cannot independently carry the full ethical responsibility for how human stories are communicated.

Human Review provides an opportunity to pause, examine context, identify risks and consider the potential consequences of publication.

❤️

Human Judgment Before Publication

Every important communication decision should consider accuracy, context, privacy, safety, consent and human dignity.

🔎 What Human Review Examines

🔎 Accuracy

Important factual claims should be checked against appropriate sources and available evidence.

🧭 Context

Content should provide enough context to avoid misleading audiences.

🔐 Privacy

Personal and sensitive information should be reviewed before publication or distribution.

🛡️ Safety

Review should consider foreseeable risks to people, communities and contributors.

❤️ Dignity

People should never be reduced to content, statistics or sensational imagery.

🤝 Consent

Appropriate consent and permissions should be considered wherever people are identifiable.

📝 The Human Review Process

1️⃣ Understand

Understand what the content is, why it exists, who it concerns and who may receive it.

2️⃣ Verify

Examine important claims, sources, dates, identities and relevant supporting information.

3️⃣ Contextualize

Consider whether the audience has enough context to understand the material accurately.

4️⃣ Assess Risk

Identify possible privacy, safety, reputational or humanitarian consequences.

5️⃣ Revise

Correct, clarify, remove or modify material when necessary.

6️⃣ Approve

Approve content for its intended use only when the responsible reviewer considers it appropriate.

🎥 Media Review

Human Review applies to every major media format, not only written material.

📷 Photography

Review identity, context, consent, privacy, dignity and potential risks before publication.

🎥 Video

Examine footage, editing, captions, sound, identities, locations and overall representation.

🎧 Audio

Review voices, recordings, statements, editing and sensitive information.

📰 Publications

Examine articles, reports, books and other written material for accuracy, context and responsibility.

🤖 Human Review of AI-Assisted Content

AI-assisted material should not automatically be considered ready for publication simply because it is technically complete.

🔎 Verify AI Output

AI-generated information should be checked against appropriate sources when factual accuracy matters.

🧠 Review Meaning

Human reviewers should examine whether generated material preserves the intended meaning and context.

🔐 Protect Sensitive Data

Sensitive personal information should be handled carefully when digital tools are involved.

👤 Preserve Human Responsibility

The use of technology does not transfer editorial responsibility from people to machines.

⚖️ Ethical Questions

Human Review should also ask questions that cannot be answered by technical accuracy alone.

❓ Is It Necessary?

Does this information need to be published or distributed at all?

❓ Is It Fair?

Does the presentation represent people and circumstances fairly?

❓ Could It Harm Someone?

Are there foreseeable consequences that should change the way the material is communicated?

❓ Would More Context Help?

Could additional information prevent misunderstanding or misleading interpretation?

❓ Would I Publish It If It Were My Story?

Consider the human perspective before making the final distribution decision.

❓ Does Dignity Come First?

If distribution conflicts with human dignity, distribution should not automatically win.

🛑 When Review Should Stop Distribution

Human Review should have the authority to pause publication when significant concerns cannot be responsibly resolved.

🚫 Unverified Information

Material with significant unresolved factual concerns should not be presented as established fact.

🚫 Unnecessary Exposure

Identifying or exposing people without sufficient reason may justify withholding the material.

🚫 Serious Safety Risk

Content creating substantial foreseeable danger should be reconsidered before distribution.

🚫 Exploitative Presentation

Material that depends on humiliation, exploitation or sensationalism should be rejected or revised.

👥 Who Can Conduct Human Review?

✍️ Editors

Review structure, accuracy, context and editorial responsibility.

🎬 Producers

Review production decisions, participants and communication objectives.

🔬 Researchers

Review sources, evidence, background information and factual context.

⚖️ Ethics Reviewers

Consider privacy, dignity, consent, safety and potential consequences.

🌍 Community Representatives

Where appropriate, community perspectives can help identify cultural or contextual concerns.

❤️ Responsible Creators

Every creator has a responsibility to consider the people represented in their work.

📋 Review Principles

🔎 Evidence

Important claims should be supported by appropriate evidence or clearly identified as interpretation.

🧭 Context

Communication should preserve the circumstances necessary for meaningful understanding.

🤝 Transparency

Significant limitations, uncertainty or corrections should be communicated appropriately.

❤️ Humanity

People should remain at the centre of ethical and editorial decisions.
