Conversations

Meaningful conversations with people whose experiences, knowledge and perspectives can help us understand the human story more deeply.

The Power of Conversation

Every humanitarian story contains a human voice. Interviews create a space where people can speak, explain, remember, question and be heard.

Echoes of Humanity conversations are designed around listening rather than sensationalism, giving people room to share their experiences with dignity and context.

Interview Ethics

A conversation is not simply content. It involves another human being and therefore carries responsibility.

🛡️

Safety

Participants should not be placed at unnecessary risk because of their participation.

🔐

Privacy

Sensitive personal information should be handled carefully and responsibly.

❤️

Dignity

People should never be reduced to headlines, statistics or sensational stories.

⚖️

Context

Conversations should preserve the meaning and context of what participants are communicating.

Human Responsibility

The purpose of an interview is to listen, understand and communicate — not to exploit another person's story.

❤️

Listen Before Speaking

Every conversation should begin with respect for the person whose voice is being shared.
