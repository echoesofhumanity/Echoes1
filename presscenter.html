📰 Press Center

The official media and press communication environment of Echoes of Humanity.

The Press Center provides journalists, media organizations, researchers and public-interest communicators with access to official information, statements, media resources and verified organizational materials.

🌍 About the Press Center

The Echoes of Humanity Press Center is designed to support responsible communication between the organization and journalists, media professionals and the wider public.

Its purpose is to make relevant information accessible while protecting accuracy, context, privacy, dignity and the safety of people represented in humanitarian stories.

⚖️ Responsible Media Communication

Media communication involving humanitarian subjects carries particular responsibilities toward people, communities and audiences.

🔎 Accuracy

Information should be presented accurately and corrected when significant errors are identified.

🧭 Context

Important information should be communicated with enough context to reduce misunderstanding.

🔐 Privacy

Sensitive personal information should be protected and shared only when appropriate.

❤️ Dignity

People affected by humanitarian circumstances should never be reduced to sensational images or headlines.

🛡️ Safety

Communication should not unnecessarily expose vulnerable people or communities to additional risk.

🤝 Respect

Media relationships should be based on transparency, professionalism and mutual respect.

🧑‍💼 For Journalists

Journalists working on stories connected to Echoes of Humanity are encouraged to seek appropriate context and verify information before publication.

🔎 Verification Before Publication

Official materials are intended to support reporting, but journalists remain responsible for their own verification, editorial judgment and source assessment.
